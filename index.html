Who is quatric?

quatric is a homebrew developer who is coming up with creative and logical projects for Nintendo consoles, especially the Wii. Ran by Larsen, a veteran to homebrew development, quatric is the name for innovative, new projects by him in the wake of AI development being his sidekick. The goal is to solve some of the biggest mysteries on the Wii and other Nintendo devices with open-source projects.
