  • BabySmash

    0.4.2 · by quatric

    Let your baby smash buttons safely

    MoreLess

    A Wii port of Scott Hanselman's BabySmash. Mash any button, key or mouse button and colourful shapes, letters and numbers pop up with giggles and spoken names. Type a word and the letters line up and say it.

    Works with Wiimote (IR pointer), Nunchuk, Classic Controller, GameCube pad, Wii U Pro Controller, Wii U GamePad, DualShock 3, USB mouse and USB keyboard.

    Hold Ctrl-Shift-Alt-O (or + and - together, or all three mouse buttons) for 3 seconds for options, including Save screenshot (also F12 / Print Screen, or hold 1 and 2 for 2 seconds). Quit with Alt-F4, or hold HOME for 2 seconds.

    Original BabySmash by Scott Hanselman (MIT). Spoken clips by espeak-ng.

  • FidgetWii

    1.2.0 · by quatric

    A fidget spinner for your Wii.

    MoreLess

    Spin a fidget spinner on your TV.

    Press A to flick it, point at it and hold B to grab and spin it by hand, shake the Wii Remote or rotate an analog stick to speed it up, and hold B to brake.

    Pick from 12 colors or Rainbow with Left/Right or +/-. Your top speed, longest spin and total spins are saved to the SD card (apps/FidgetWii/record.txt).

    Works with Wii Remote, Nunchuk, Classic Controller, GameCube controller and USB keyboard.

    Spinner photo: "Yellow Fidget Spinner" by Daserino, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons.

  • GodModeWii

    0.15.1 · by quatric, cmyksoda

    GodMode9-style file manager

    MoreLess

    A text-mode, GodMode9-style file manager for the Wii and the Wii U's vWii.

    Browse the SD card, SD Gecko, every USB drive, the NAND, the SEEPROM, the disc drive and your console's keys. Copy, cut, paste and delete between devices, see free space, and edit the NAND only after the Konami code and a brick-risk disclaimer.

  • Mighty Mike

    1.1 · by quatric

    Wii port of Mighty Mike (Pangea Software / jorio)

    MoreLess

    Unofficial Wii homebrew port of Mighty Mike, Iliyas Jorio's modern reimplementation of the 1995 Pangea Software game "Mutant Mike." Rendering runs through opengx (GX-backed fixed-function OpenGL); no game logic was changed to make this run.

    CONTROLS

    GameCube controller: Control Stick - Move A - Confirm / pause menu select X - Shoot Y - Radar B - Next weapon / back out of menus L / R - Cycle weapons Start - Pause

    Wiimote + Nunchuk: Nunchuk stick - Move A - Confirm B - Radar Z - Shoot C - Next weapon Minus - Previous weapon Plus - Pause Home - Exit to the Homebrew Channel

    Wiimote only (held sideways, d-pad on the left): D-pad - Move A - Confirm B - Radar 2 - Shoot 1 - Next weapon Minus - Previous weapon Plus - Pause Home - Exit to the Homebrew Channel

    USB keyboard (if plugged in): arrow keys, WASD, and the game's other default PC keybindings all work as-is.

    Downloadmeta.xmlicon.png

  • Mini vMac Wii

    1.1.0

    Classic Macintosh emulator

    MoreLess

    Mini vMac emulating a Macintosh 128K, 512Ke, Plus, SE, Classic, II or IIx, filling the whole TV screen. Pick the Mac when the app starts; HOME > Switch Mac model changes it later.

    Put the ROMs in sd:/apps/MinivMacWii/ (Mac128K.ROM, vMac.ROM, MacSE.ROM, Classic.ROM, MacII.ROM, MacIIx.ROM) along with your disk images (disk1.dsk, disk2.dsk, ... are inserted at start; a model's own folder, such as II/, is looked in first; HOME opens a menu to insert others).

    Wii Remote: point and press A or B to use the mouse, D-Pad for arrow keys, 1 = command, 2 = option, + = on-screen keyboard, - = display mode, HOME = menu. Nunchuk, Classic Controller, GameCube pads and USB keyboards and mice work too.

    Downloadmeta.xmlicon.png

  • R

    RecordMii

    0.2 · by quatric

    Record the Wii Speak or a USB mic to WAV

    MoreLess

    Records audio from a Wii Speak or a USB microphone to 16-bit mono WAV files in sd:/wiispeak.

    USB microphones (USB Audio Class, the plug-and-play kind) need nothing extra and record at up to 48 kHz. The Wii Speak requires pmic_firm.bin (the Wii Speak's boot firmware, extracted from an RVL SDK pmic.a) alongside this app. Rate, mic gain and the echo canceller are adjustable.

    A: start/stop recording. LEFT/RIGHT: sample rate. UP/DOWN: gain. B: echo canceller. HOME: exit.

    Downloadmeta.xmlicon.png

  • Wii Mobiclip Player

    1.3 · by quatric

    Play Mobiclip, THP, and Flipnote videos

    MoreLess

    A Wii homebrew app that plays Wii Mobiclip files directly on hardware. No transcoding is used, every video is decoded on the Wii. It supports ADPCM, FastAudio, PCM, and Vorbis audio, plus THP video and PPM/KWZ Flipnote animations.

    Supported controllers include Wii Remote, Classic Controller, GameCube Controller, Wii U GamePad, Wii U Pro Controller, and wired DualShock 3.

    Controls:

    Browser: - +Control Pad Up/Down - move selection - A - open folder / play file - B - parent folder - HOME - exit

    During playback: - A / + - pause / resume - B / HOME - stop, back to browser
